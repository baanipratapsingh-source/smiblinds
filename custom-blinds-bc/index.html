Measuring Guide

How to Measure for
Custom Blinds

The part most people worry about. You'll do it with us on the phone, but here's what it involves so you know what to expect.

1. Choose inside or outside mount

Inside mount: the blind sits inside the window frame for a clean, built-in look. Needs enough depth in the frame.
Outside mount: the blind is fixed to the wall or trim and covers the whole window. Best for shallow frames and for blocking the most light.

2. Measure the width three times

For an inside mount, measure across the top, middle and bottom of the opening. Frames are rarely perfectly square, so we use the smallest number. Measure in millimetres or to the nearest 1/8″.

3. Measure the height three times

Measure the left side, middle and right side, from the top of the opening to the sill. For an outside mount, add overlap on each side and above for better privacy and light control.

4. Note anything in the way

Window cranks, handles, alarm sensors, tile edges and trim can all get in the way of a blind. Mention them, or show us in a photo, and we'll plan around them.

Tip: Use a steel tape measure, not a cloth one, and measure every window separately — even windows that look identical are often a few millimetres different.

Common Questions

Custom Blinds BC FAQ

Do you sell custom blinds outside Metro Vancouver?

Yes. We serve all of British Columbia. In the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland towns like Chilliwack, Mission, Maple Ridge and Delta, we measure and install in your home. Everywhere else — Vancouver Island, the Okanagan, Kamloops, the Kootenays, Northern BC and small towns in between — we consult by phone or video, guide you through measuring and send your custom blinds to you.

Can I get custom blinds in Kelowna, Victoria or Prince George?

Yes. Call 778-932-0696 or send the form with your town. We'll set up a free phone or video consultation, help you choose the right blind for each window, walk you through measuring and check every number before your blinds are made.

I live in a small town. Is that a problem?

No. That's exactly who this service is for. Because you measure with our guidance instead of waiting for someone to drive out, it doesn't matter how far you are from Vancouver.

What if I measure wrong?

That's the reason we measure with you instead of leaving you to it. We go through each window step by step, ask about anything that could get in the way, and check your numbers before anything is made. If something looks off, we'll ask you to re-measure that window first.

Can I install custom blinds myself?

Yes. Most of our styles mount with brackets on the frame or wall, and you only need a drill, a screwdriver and a level. Your blinds come with hardware and instructions, and you can call us with questions while you install.

How long does it take to get custom blinds outside the Lower Mainland?

Every blind is made to order once you approve your quote. We'll confirm your timeline, including delivery to your town, at that point.

How much do custom blinds cost?

It depends on how many windows you're covering, their size, the fabric you choose and whether you go manual or motorized. Once we have your measurements, you get an exact, no-obligation quote before you commit to anything.

Which blinds are best for BC's climate?

It depends on where you are. On the coast and Vancouver Island, light-filtering fabrics keep rooms bright through grey winters. In the Okanagan and Kamloops, solar roller shades cut summer heat and glare. In Northern BC and the mountains, honeycomb blinds hold heat in and blackout blinds help with very long summer days.

I'm in Vancouver, Burnaby or Surrey. Is this page for me?

Probably not. If you're in a city we have a page for, we'll come to your home, measure and install. Go to our pages for Vancouver, North Vancouver, West Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Coquitlam, Surrey, Langley or Abbotsford.
