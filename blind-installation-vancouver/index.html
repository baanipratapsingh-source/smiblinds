Blind Measuring & Installation,
Done by One Team

Most problems with new blinds aren't the blind itself — they're the fit and the install. A blind ordered a centimetre too wide won't sit inside the frame. Brackets that aren't level make a shade roll crooked. Blackout fabric with gaps at the edges lets light pour in anyway.

That's why we handle the whole job. The same company that measures your windows makes your blinds to those measurements and installs them, so nothing gets lost between a measuring sheet and the installer. You don't measure anything yourself, and you don't have to coordinate a separate installer.

This page is about how that service works: the free measuring visit, installation day, what's included in your quote and what affects the price. If you're still choosing a style, start with the blinds we install below.

for a Perfect Fit

Every installation starts with a free visit. A specialist comes to you, measures every window and brings real fabric samples so you can see them in your own light.

📐

What we look at during the visit

The size of each window, how deep the frame is, anything in the way — handles, cranks, trim, alarm sensors — and how you use each room. That tells us which style and which mount will actually work.

⬜

Inside mount or outside mount

An inside mount sits within the window frame for a clean, built-in look but needs enough frame depth. An outside mount covers the frame and overlaps the wall, which blocks more light at the edges and suits shallow frames and patio doors. We’ll recommend one for each window.

📏

Windows that aren’t perfectly square

Older Vancouver homes and even new builds often have frames that are slightly out of square. We measure each window at more than one point so the blind is sized to the narrowest point and doesn’t bind or leave a visible gap.

✅

You leave with a fixed quote

Before anything is ordered, you get a fixed, no-obligation quote based on our measurements — not a ballpark from a website form.

What affects your price

📐

Number and size of windows

Larger windows and more of them mean more material and more installation time.

🎨

Style and fabric

Zebra, roller, blackout, honeycomb, faux wood and vertical blinds are priced differently, and fabric choice matters within each style.

⚡

Manual or motorized

Motorized blinds cost more than manual ones. Hardwired, battery, rechargeable and solar options are available, depending on whether there’s power near the window.

🚪

Specialty openings

Wide patio doors, very tall windows and openings split into several shades take more planning and hardware than a standard window.

What to watch for in other quotes: if a blinds quote doesn’t mention measuring or installation, ask whether they’re extra. With SMI, the fixed quote you approve already includes both, and there’s no obligation to go ahead. Our guide to choosing the right blind installers has more questions worth asking.

Common Questions

Blind Installation FAQ

Do you offer free measuring for blind installation?

Yes. A specialist visits your home, measures every window and brings real fabric samples so you can compare options in your own light — free, with no pressure or obligation to buy.

How does your measure & install process work?

Book a free in-home visit. We measure your windows, show you samples and give you a quote. Once you approve it, your blinds are custom-made to those measurements, and our team comes back to install, level, test and set up everything — including any motorized controls — usually in a single visit.

How long does a blind installation appointment take?

It depends on how many windows you're doing, but most installations are completed the same day our team arrives, and a whole home is often finished in one visit. As two of our Google reviewers put it: one living room was “done in two hours,” and another family had “11 windows done in one day.”

How soon can you install after measuring?

Every blind is made to order after you approve your quote, so installation is booked once your blinds are ready. Fabrication time depends on the style and fabric, and we'll confirm your expected timeline when you approve your quote.

How much does blind installation cost in Vancouver?

Installation is included in your quote rather than charged as a separate fee, so the number you approve covers measuring, your custom blinds and the install. What changes the total is the number and size of your windows, the style and fabric, and whether you choose manual or motorized operation. We give you a fixed, no-obligation quote in your home before anything is ordered.

Do you take down and remove my old blinds?

Yes. When we install your new blinds we take down the old ones and can take them away for you — just let us know at the consultation.

Can you install blinds I bought somewhere else?

Our measure & install service is built around the custom blinds we make for your windows. If you've already bought blinds elsewhere, give us a call and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.

Can you install motorized blinds in the same visit?

Yes. Any style we offer can be motorized, and we install it and pair your remote, app and smart-home devices — Alexa, Google Home or Apple HomeKit — in the same visit.

Do you install blinds in condos and strata buildings?

Yes. We regularly install in Metro Vancouver condos and new-build homes, including buildings with strata guidelines on exterior-facing window coverings, and we'll help you choose a compliant option.

What should I do before the installer arrives?

Clear window sills and move furniture or fragile items a little way back from the windows so there's room to work, and keep pets in another room if you can. That's all — we'll take care of the rest.

Do you serve all of Metro Vancouver?

We install across Vancouver, West Vancouver, North Vancouver, Burnaby, Surrey, Richmond, Coquitlam, Langley, Delta and Abbotsford, with free in-home measurement in every city we serve.
