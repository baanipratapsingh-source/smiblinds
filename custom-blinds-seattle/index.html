Seattle Weather

Energy-Efficient Shades for
Seattle's Rain & Sun

Seattle's reputation is rain, but your windows deal with three very different seasons.

🌧️

Grey, Wet Winters

From October to March, daylight is short and precious. You want privacy without making rooms darker, and cold glass can make rooms near windows uncomfortable.

Best fitLight-filtering roller shades and zebra blinds; cellular shades for insulation.

☀️

Hot Summer Afternoons

Seattle summers are dry and sunny, and heat waves are getting more common — Seattle hit 108°F in June 2021. Many homes have no air conditioning, so blocking sun at the glass matters.

Best fitSolar shades on west-facing windows to cut heat and glare while keeping the view, or cellular shades.

🌅

Early Sunrises

Around the summer solstice the sun rises a little after 5 a.m. and sets after 9 p.m. in Seattle. Light-filtering fabric won't keep a bedroom dark that early.

Best fitBlackout shades for bedrooms and nurseries, mounted to minimize light gaps.

Measuring Basics

Measuring Your Windows
for Custom Shades

You'll do this with us on the phone, so there's no need to get it perfect alone. Here's what's involved.

Inside or outside mount?

An inside mount sits within the window frame for a built-in look and needs enough frame depth. An outside mount attaches above the window and overlaps the sides — better for shallow frames and for maximum darkness.

Width: top, middle, bottom

Most frames aren't perfectly square, so measure the width in three places and use the narrowest. Measure to the nearest 1/8 inch with a steel tape.

Height: left, center, right

Measure from the top of the opening to the sill in three places. For outside mounts, we'll tell you how much overlap to add for privacy and light control.

Obstructions

Crank handles, door levers, alarm sensors and deep trim can all affect the fit. Point them out or send a photo and we'll plan around them.

Common Questions

Custom Blinds Seattle FAQ

Do you serve Seattle and the greater Seattle area?

Yes. In Whatcom County near the border — Bellingham, Blaine, Birch Bay, Ferndale and Lynden — we measure and install in your home. Across the rest of Greater Seattle, including Seattle, Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond, Everett, Renton, Kent and Tacoma, we consult by phone or video, guide you through measuring and ship your custom blinds to you.

Are you a local Seattle company?

No, and we'd rather be upfront about it. SMI Blinds is based in the Lower Mainland of British Columbia, a short drive north of the border. We've completed 1500+ homes in the Pacific Northwest climate and serve Washington customers by phone, video and shipping, with in-home service near the border.

Can someone come to my home in Seattle or Bellevue to measure?

For the Seattle metro, we work by phone or video and guide you through measuring yourself, checking every number before anything is made. In-home measuring and installation is available near the border in Whatcom County. If you're in between, such as Skagit County, call 778-932-0696 to ask about your address.

What if I measure my windows wrong?

That's why we don't leave you to it. We go through each window with you, ask about anything that could get in the way, and review every measurement before your order goes into production. If a number looks off, we'll ask you to re-measure that window first.

Can I install the blinds myself?

Yes. Most of our shades and blinds mount with brackets on the frame or wall, and you only need a drill, a screwdriver and a level. They arrive with hardware and instructions, and you can call us with questions while you install.

How long does it take to get custom blinds in Washington?

Every blind is made to order once you approve your quote. We'll confirm your timeline, including shipping to your address, at that point.

How much do custom blinds cost in Seattle?

It depends on how many windows you're covering, their size, the fabric you choose and whether you go manual or motorized. Once we have your measurements, you get an exact, no-obligation quote before you commit to anything.

What are the best blinds for Seattle weather?

Most Seattle homes need a mix. Light-filtering roller shades or zebra blinds keep rooms bright through grey winters, solar shades cut heat and glare on west-facing windows in summer, blackout shades keep bedrooms dark through early summer sunrises, and cellular shades add insulation year-round.

What are the best window coverings for west-facing windows?

Solar shades are usually the first choice: they cut glare and much of the heat at the glass while keeping your view of the Sound or the Olympics. Keep in mind solar and light-filtering fabrics can be see-through at night with the lights on, so bedrooms usually get blackout fabric instead.

Do you offer motorized and smart home blinds?

Yes. Most of our styles can be motorized, with remote, wall-switch, app or voice control through Alexa, Google Home or Apple HomeKit — useful for two-story great rooms and wide condo windows. See motorized blinds.

Do you sell plantation shutters or Roman shades?

Not at the moment. We focus on roller and solar shades, zebra blinds, blackout shades, cellular shades, faux wood blinds, vertical blinds and motorized options. If you're not sure which fits a window, we'll help you choose during the consultation.
