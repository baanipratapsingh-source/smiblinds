Our Coquitlam Service

Serving Every Coquitlam Neighbourhood,
From Burke Mountain to Maillardville

Coquitlam splits into four distinct zones, and we outfit all of them. In the northeast, Burke Mountain's new-build detached homes and townhomes make up Coquitlam's fastest-growing family community; just north and west, Westwood Plateau and Eagle Ridge's larger-lot hillside estates back onto forest and golf-course views. Central and west, the Town Centre, North Coquitlam and Coquitlam West (Burquitlam) condo corridor runs the length of the Evergreen SkyTrain line. To the south, Central Coquitlam, Maillardville and Harbour Chines keep an established, character-home feel around Como Lake and the historic French-Canadian village of Maillardville.

Wherever you are in the city, every blind we install is custom fabricated to your exact windows — not an off-the-shelf size. One of our specialists visits your home or condo, measures every opening, and brings real fabric and slat samples so you can compare options in your own light, free of charge and with zero obligation.

Common Questions

Coquitlam Blinds FAQ

Do you install blinds throughout Coquitlam?

Yes. We install custom blinds and window coverings across every Coquitlam zone — from the new-build homes of Burke Mountain and hillside estates of Westwood Plateau, to the Town Centre, North Coquitlam and Coquitlam West condo corridor, to the established streets of Central Coquitlam, Maillardville and Harbour Chines — with free in-home measurement included everywhere we serve.

Can you install blinds in new-build homes on Burke Mountain?

Yes. Burke Mountain is one of our most active zones — we regularly fit blinds and motorized shades in new-build detached homes and townhomes, including the larger windows and mountain-view glass common in newer construction there.

Can you install blinds in high-rise condos near Coquitlam Centre or the SkyTrain stations?

Yes. High-rise and mid-rise condo installs are a regular part of our work in Town Centre, North Coquitlam and Coquitlam West — we're comfortable with strata booking procedures, elevator restrictions and tight installation windows, and we fit the floor-to-ceiling glass common in newer towers near the Evergreen Line stations.

Do strata rules affect what window coverings I can install in my Coquitlam condo?

Some Coquitlam strata corporations have bylaws around the exterior appearance of window coverings, particularly the colour or backing visible from outside the building. We can advise on compliant options during your free consultation, and most buildings allow full freedom of choice on the interior-facing side.

Can you match blinds to heritage character homes in Maillardville?

Yes. Maillardville's heritage French-Canadian character homes often have older, non-standard window openings and trim — we measure and fabricate every blind to your exact window, so character openings are handled with the same precision as a brand-new build.

Do you offer motorized blinds for new condo towers?

Yes. Motorized roller, zebra and blackout blinds are a popular upgrade in Town Centre and North Coquitlam's new-construction towers — remote, wall-switch, app and voice control are all available, with Alexa and Google Home integration, and no cords to manage in a compact unit.

How much do custom blinds cost in Coquitlam?

Pricing depends on window size and count, fabric or slat choice, and whether you choose manual or motorized operation, so every home is different. We provide a free in-home measurement and consultation so you get an accurate, no-obligation quote before committing to anything.

Do you serve Westwood Plateau and Eagle Ridge?

Yes. We install custom blinds throughout Westwood Plateau and Eagle Ridge, including the larger windows and view-oriented glass common in these hillside, golf-course-adjacent neighbourhoods, with the same free in-home measurement and full product range available across the rest of Coquitlam.

How fast can you install blinds in Coquitlam?

In many cases, once your custom blinds are fabricated to your measurements, our installation team can complete a full home or condo in Coquitlam in a single visit. Call 778-932-0696 or request a free estimate to check current availability in your neighbourhood.
