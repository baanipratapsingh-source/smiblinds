Common Questions

Custom Blinds Vancouver FAQ

What types of custom blinds do you offer?

We design, fabricate and install zebra blinds, roller shades, blackout blinds, faux wood blinds, honeycomb (cellular) blinds and vertical blinds — all custom-made to your exact window measurements, with motorized and smart-home options available across the full range.

What areas of Metro Vancouver do you serve?

We serve homeowners across Vancouver, Surrey, Burnaby, Langley, Coquitlam, Richmond, North Vancouver, Delta and Abbotsford — with free in-home measurement and professional installation available in every city.

Do you offer free in-home consultations?

Yes. A specialist visits your home, measures every window precisely, and brings real fabric samples so you can see and feel your options in your own lighting — completely free, with no pressure or obligation to buy.

How quickly can you install custom blinds?

Most installations across Metro Vancouver are completed the same day our team arrives once your custom blinds are fabricated and ready — fast, clean and fully guaranteed.

Can you install motorized smart home blinds?

Yes. Any style we offer can be motorized with remote, wall-switch or smartphone control, and integrates with Alexa, Google Home and Apple HomeKit — a popular upgrade for large windows, skylights and hard-to-reach spaces.

Do you install blinds in condos and new builds?

Yes, we regularly work in Metro Vancouver condos and new-build homes, including buildings with strata guidelines on exterior-facing window coverings — we’ll help you choose a compliant, great-looking option.

What is the price range for custom blinds in Vancouver?

Pricing depends on window size, fabric, and whether you choose manual or motorized operation, so every home is different. Your free in-home estimate gives you an accurate, no-obligation quote before you commit to anything.

How does your process work from consultation to installation?

Book a free in-home visit, and a specialist measures your windows and shows you real fabric samples on the spot. Once you approve your order, we fabricate your custom blinds and schedule a professional, same-day installation — backed by our satisfaction guarantee.
