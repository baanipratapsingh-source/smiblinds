Our Abbotsford Service

Window Blinds for Abbotsford,
Chosen for the Fraser Valley

Abbotsford sits in the Fraser Valley, just east of Langley and outside Metro Vancouver. It is far enough inland that the ocean does less to even out the weather: summers get hotter and winters colder than they do in Vancouver. On June 28, 2021, during the heat dome, Abbotsford recorded 42.9°C while Vancouver peaked at 31.7°C. In winter, Environment Canada issues Arctic outflow warnings for the Fraser Valley, when cold, dry wind pours down the valley and pushes wind chills well below zero.

That changes which fabric belongs on which window, so we don't start from a catalogue. A specialist visits your home, measures every window and brings real fabric and slat samples so you can see each option in your own light — whether that's a hillside living room in Auguston, a family home in Clearbrook or a farmhouse window out in Bradner. The measurement and consultation are free, with no obligation. Closer to the city? See our custom blinds in Langley page.

Fraser Valley Conditions

Blinds for Abbotsford's
Hotter Summers & Colder Winters

Three things about Abbotsford are worth weighing before you pick a fabric. Here's how each one changes what we'd suggest.

☀️

Hot Summer Afternoons

Inland summer heat builds through the afternoon, and west- and south-facing rooms take the worst of it. Blocking sun at the glass keeps more of that heat out of the room than an open window will.

Best fitSolar roller shades to cut glare and heat while keeping the view, or honeycomb blinds, whose cells add a layer of insulation.

❄️

Cold Snaps & Outflow Winds

When Arctic air flows down the valley, older and single-pane windows feel cold to sit beside. A blind can't replace a good window, but a cellular shade closed at night traps a layer of air against the glass.

Best fitDouble cell honeycomb blinds on bedroom and family-room windows, mounted to sit close to the glass.

⛰️

Big Valley & Mountain Views

Hillside homes on Eagle Mountain and in Auguston are built around large view windows. You want to control glare without covering the view you paid for, and some of that glass is hard to reach.

Best fitZebra blinds or solar shades to keep the view, and motorized blinds for tall or out-of-reach windows.

Common Questions

Blinds Abbotsford BC FAQ

Do you travel to Abbotsford for in-home measurements?

Yes. Abbotsford is one of the cities on our service list, and we offer the same free in-home measurement and consultation there as everywhere else we serve. Call 778-932-0696 or send the form to check availability for your part of Abbotsford.

What are the best blinds for hot summer afternoons in Abbotsford?

For rooms that take strong afternoon sun, solar roller shades are usually the first choice: they cut glare and much of the heat at the glass while keeping your view. Honeycomb blinds are another good option because their cells add a layer of insulation. Keep in mind that solar and light-filtering fabrics can be see-through at night with the lights on, so bedrooms usually get blackout fabric instead.

Which blinds help most during cold winters and Arctic outflow?

Double cell honeycomb blinds are the strongest insulating option we offer. Closed at night and mounted close to the glass, they trap a layer of air between the room and a cold window. They help, especially on older or single-pane windows, but they don't replace good windows or weatherstripping.

Can you fit blinds in new builds in Auguston or Eagle Mountain?

Yes. New-build homes are measured in person like any other, including tall great-room glass, view windows and stairwell windows. Motorized blinds are a popular choice for windows you can't easily reach.

Do you install blinds on acreages and farm properties?

Yes. Rural homes and farmhouses often have non-standard window sizes, which is exactly why every blind we install is measured on site and made to fit. Faux wood blinds are a practical choice for mudrooms, kitchens and other rooms that see moisture.

Roller, zebra or honeycomb blinds: which is better for an Abbotsford home?

It depends on the room. Roller shades give one clean sheet of fabric: solar to keep the view, light-filtering for a soft glow, or blackout for full darkness. Zebra blinds let you shift between view and privacy without raising the blind. Honeycomb blinds insulate best, which matters in a climate with hotter summers and colder winters than Vancouver's. Many homes use a mix, and we bring samples of all three to your consultation.

Do you offer blackout and motorized blinds in Abbotsford?

Yes. Our full range is available in Abbotsford, including blackout blinds for bedrooms and nurseries and motorized blinds with remote, wall-switch, app or voice control through Alexa, Google Home or Apple HomeKit.

Can you fit blinds on patio and sliding glass doors?

Yes. Vertical blinds, zebra blinds, roller shades and motorized options can all be made for sliding doors. During the visit we measure the handle depth and the sliding panel, then choose the mount so the blind hangs clear of the handle and doesn't rub when the door slides. See patio & sliding door blinds.

How much do custom blinds cost in Abbotsford?

Pricing depends on how many windows you're covering, their size, the fabric or slat you choose, and whether you go manual or motorized, so every home is different. The free in-home measurement gives you an accurate, no-obligation quote before you commit to anything.

How long does it take to get blinds installed in Abbotsford?

Every blind is made to order once you approve your quote, and we'll confirm your timeline at that point. Call 778-932-0696 to check current availability for a measurement appointment in Abbotsford.
