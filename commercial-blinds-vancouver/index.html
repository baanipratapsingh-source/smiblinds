Why Commercial Spaces

A blind at home is used by one household. A blind in a business is raised and lowered by staff, customers and cleaners every day, and it’s part of how your space looks to everyone who walks in.

Built for daily use

Business windows get opened and closed far more often than windows at home. Simple, sturdy mechanisms and durable fabrics hold up better and are easier to keep running.

🔒

Privacy where it’s needed

Meeting rooms, exam rooms and street-level offices need privacy without making the whole space feel closed off.

🌙

Installed without disrupting the business

Offices

Office Blinds for
Workspaces & Meeting Rooms

Office window blinds have three jobs: keep glare off screens, give meeting rooms privacy when they need it, and make a full floor look consistent. Roller shades are the usual choice, often with a heavier fabric in meeting rooms.

💻

Glare control for screens and workstations

A light-filtering or solar roller shade softens daylight so screens stay readable without switching on every light. Solar fabric also keeps a view to the outside, so the office doesn’t feel closed in.

👥

Privacy for meeting rooms and glass offices

Meeting rooms and offices facing the street or a neighbouring building can use a more private fabric than the open floor. Blackout fabric also makes screens and projectors easier to see.

📏

One consistent look across a full floor

We measure every window, then order and install them together so the fabric, colour and hem height line up from one end of the floor to the other.

What usually works in offices

  • Solar or light-filtering roller shades for open work areas
  • Blackout blinds for meeting and training rooms
  • Vertical blinds for wide windows and glass doors

Restaurants & Cafés

Restaurant &
Café Blinds

Restaurant window blinds need to handle sun on diners at the busiest times of day, survive a busy room and still suit the look you’ve built.

🌞

Afternoon sun and glare on diners

West- and south-facing windows can make window tables uncomfortable at lunch and dinner. A solar roller shade cuts glare from direct sun while guests can still see out.

🧽

Easy-to-clean, durable fabrics

In a restaurant, blinds get touched and splashed. Smooth roller fabrics are simple to wipe down, and there are no slats to collect grease and dust.

🕯️

Keeping the room’s look and ambience

Fabric colour and opacity change how a room feels, day and night. We bring samples when we measure so you can see them in your own space and light.

What usually works in restaurants

  • Solar roller shades for street-facing and patio windows
  • Light-filtering roller shades for a softer, warmer dining room
  • Vertical blinds for wide glass doors to a patio

Retail

Retail Store
Blinds

In a store, the windows do some of the selling. Retail store blinds need to protect what’s on display without hiding the shop from the street.

🛍️

Protecting products from sun fading

Strong sun through a shop window can fade packaging, fabrics and displays over time. Solar roller shades filter UV and glare during the brightest part of the day.

🏪

Keeping the storefront visible and inviting

Solar fabric lets you see out, but in bright daylight it also cuts how much passers-by see in. That’s why it usually works best to lower shades only while the sun is on the glass, and keep them up the rest of the day.

What usually works in stores

  • Solar roller shades for display windows and storefronts
  • Light-filtering or blackout roller shades for back offices and staff rooms

Retail is work we already do. One recent commercial job was a perfume shop in Coquitlam.

Rental & Strata

Blinds for Rental &
Strata Buildings

If you’re choosing blinds for rental property or a strata building, you want the same look in every unit, blinds that survive tenant turnover, and one company that can handle the whole order.

🏢

One standard look across many units

Using one product, fabric and colour in every unit keeps the building looking uniform from outside, which some strata bylaws require, and makes replacements simple.

🔄

Durable, low-maintenance options for tenant turnover

Simple roller shades and vertical blinds are easy to clean between tenants and have fewer parts to break than more decorative styles.

📦

Large orders and whole buildings

We take on large orders, up to a whole building. And with no minimum job size, a single suite between tenants is fine too.

What usually works in rental & strata

  • Roller shades as a simple standard for every unit
  • Vertical blinds for sliding balcony doors

Commercial Roller Shades

Commercial Roller Shades:
What Most Businesses Choose

When businesses ask us for commercial blinds, roller shades are what most of them choose. They’re simple to operate, easy to clean and look tidy in any space, from a single office to a full restaurant.

Commercial roller shades come in three main fabric types: solar fabric that cuts glare and UV while keeping your view, light-filtering fabric that softens daylight, and blackout fabric for full privacy and darkness. It’s common to mix them, for example solar in the main space and blackout in meeting rooms.

See fabrics, colours and photos on our roller shades page.

Common Questions

Commercial Blinds FAQ

How much do commercial blinds cost?

Every commercial quote is based on your window sizes, how many windows there are and the products you choose, so we don’t publish a price list. Send us the quote form or call 778-932-0696 and we’ll book a visit to measure and give you a quote.

Do you have a minimum job size?

No. There’s no minimum job size. We’ll do a single office window, one suite between tenants or a whole building.

Can you handle large orders, like a full office floor or a whole building?

Yes. We take on large commercial orders as well as small ones. We measure every window, order everything together so it matches, and plan the installation around how your business runs.

Can you install after hours or on weekends?

Yes. We can schedule installation after hours or on a weekend so you don’t have to close or work around installers. Tell us what works for you when you request your quote.

How long does a commercial blinds order take?

About 7–8 business days from when you place your order to installation. We’ll confirm the timeline with your quote.

What are the best blinds for an office?

For most offices, roller shades: light-filtering or solar fabric in open work areas to cut screen glare, and blackout fabric in meeting rooms for privacy and presentations. Vertical blinds can work well on wide windows and glass doors. We’ll recommend options for each room when we measure.

What blinds work best for restaurants and cafés?

Solar roller shades are a common choice for dining rooms with strong afternoon sun, because they cut glare while guests can still see out. Roller fabrics are also easy to wipe clean, which matters in a busy room.

Are roller shades a good choice for businesses?

Yes. Roller shades are what most of our commercial clients choose. They’re simple to use, easy to clean, look tidy across many windows, and come in solar, light-filtering and blackout fabrics.

Do you work with rental and strata buildings?

Yes. We work with landlords, property managers and strata buildings, from a single suite between tenants to the same blinds across many units. If your building has rules about window coverings, share them with us and we’ll quote options that fit.
