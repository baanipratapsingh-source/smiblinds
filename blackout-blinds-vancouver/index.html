Our Blackout Blinds

Premium Blackout Shades for Every
Metro Vancouver Home

"Blackout blinds" is what most homeowners search for, but technically it's the shade's fabric that determines light block, not the blind style itself. True blackout fabrics block 99–100% of light, while "room darkening" fabrics block roughly 95–99% — enough to dim a room noticeably without full darkness. We carry both, across roller, cellular and other shade styles, so you get exactly the level of light control your room needs.

No two homes are the same, so we don't sell off-the-shelf sizes. One of our specialists visits your home, measures every window, and brings real blackout, cellular and room-darkening fabric samples so you can compare light-block levels in your own light — free of charge and with zero obligation.

Common Questions

Blackout Blinds FAQ

What is the difference between blackout blinds and room darkening blinds?

"Blackout" blinds use an opaque, light-blocking fabric or backing that blocks essentially all direct light through the material itself, while "room darkening" fabrics block roughly 95–99% of light — enough to noticeably dim a room but not achieve full darkness. Both terms get used loosely by shoppers, so we always confirm which level of light control you actually need before recommending a fabric.

Do blackout blinds completely block all light?

The fabric itself blocks nearly all direct light, but some light can still enter around the edges of the blind through the small gap between the fabric and the window frame — this is normal with any inside-mount shade. For a true zero-light-gap result, common for nurseries and shift-worker bedrooms, we recommend side channel tracks or an outside-mount frame with a cassette valance that closes off that edge gap.

What are the best blackout blinds for a bedroom?

Blackout roller shades are the most popular choice for Vancouver bedrooms — sleek, easy to operate, and available in a wide range of colors and textures. Blackout cellular (honeycomb) shades are a strong alternative if you also want extra insulation, since the honeycomb structure traps air against the window.

Are blackout blinds good for nurseries and babies?

Yes — blackout blinds are one of the most requested products for nurseries, since consistent darkness supports better daytime and nighttime sleep for babies and toddlers. We recommend cordless operation for any nursery or child's room for safety, and can pair the blind with side channels if you want to eliminate the edge light gap completely.

Can blackout blinds be motorized?

Yes. Every blackout blind we fabricate can be upgraded to a motorized, cordless system operated by remote, wall switch or smartphone app, with Alexa, Google Home and Apple HomeKit integration available — a popular option for large windows, media rooms and hard-to-reach skylights.

Are blackout blinds a good choice for shift workers?

Yes — shift workers who sleep during daylight hours are one of the groups that benefits most from blackout blinds, since consistent darkness helps the body maintain a regular sleep schedule regardless of the time of day outside. We often pair blackout fabric with side channel tracks for shift-worker bedrooms specifically to close off any residual edge light.

Do blackout blinds help reduce heat and noise?

Blackout fabrics are typically thicker and more tightly woven than light-filtering fabrics, which gives them some secondary benefit as heat and sound buffers compared to a sheer or light-filtering shade — though they aren't a substitute for dedicated insulation or soundproofing. Cellular (honeycomb) blackout shades offer the strongest thermal benefit of our blackout options, thanks to their air-trapping cell structure.

How much do custom blackout blinds cost installed in Vancouver?

Pricing depends on window size, fabric choice, mounting style (inside mount vs. side channels), and whether you choose manual or motorized operation, so every home is different. We provide a free in-home measurement and consultation so you get an accurate, no-obligation quote before committing to anything.
